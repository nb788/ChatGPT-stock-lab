# Stock Project Research Resume Gate

This repository is the shared public data-engineering bridge for the entire
Stocks project. It mirrors—but does not replace—the authoritative gate in
`nb788/stock-lab-runtime` at:

```text
research_only/project_resume_gate_v1.json
```

## Current research state

`BLOCKED_PREWORK`

Public source acquisition, transport, parsing, quality assurance, and canonical
export may continue because those activities help resolve the prerequisites.
They do not authorize research consumption.

Until the authoritative runtime gate reports `OPEN`, bridge outputs may not be
used to begin or continue:

- new historical backtests;
- indicator or feature searches;
- challenger tuning;
- prospective challenger decisions;
- holdout access;
- model promotion.

Every stock-related chat, agent, notebook, or Work session must validate the
authoritative gate before producing new empirical conclusions. A missing,
stale, or blocked gate report means the work is non-decision-usable and must be
restricted to remediation.

Opening the research gate will not authorize an order, activate a corrected
execution contract, open a holdout, or approve a production model. Those remain
separate controls.
